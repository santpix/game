02 — HERITAGE

Irish heritage.
Modern millinery.

Tradition is not nostalgia. It is technique, discipline and an instinct for proportion carried forward by hand.

Marie’s work brings old-world craft into a modern wardrobe: sculptural wirework, hand-finished flowers, couture headbands and one-of-a-kind forms created for weddings, racing season, Royal Ascot, the Kentucky Derby and every occasion that calls for presence.
